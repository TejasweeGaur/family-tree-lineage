Privacy Policy

Vanshavali · Family Tree & Lineage · Last updated 1 October 2026

Who runs this

Vanshavali (Family Tree & Lineage) is a personal, non-commercial project created and operated by Tejaswee Gaur. Questions or requests: tejasweegaur111@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to identify you, show who is signed in, and let the admins of a tree you have joined see who has access to it.
  • What families add to their tree. Names, dates and places of birth, marriage and death, occupations, biographies, relationships, photographs and scanned documents. This can include information about living relatives, so please only add what the people concerned would be comfortable with.
  • Small settings in your browser. Your sign-in session, which tree you last opened, and whether you have seen the quick tour are kept in your browser's local storage.

Who can see it

Each family tree is private. Only people who have been invited to a tree can see it. Viewers can browse; admins can also add, edit and delete. Photographs and documents are kept in private storage and are shown through links that expire after one hour.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no selling or sharing of your data.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Google account data is used only for signing in and showing who you are, and for nothing else.

Where it is stored

Data and files are stored with Supabase, which provides the database, sign-in and file storage. The website is served by Cloudflare. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them.

Deleting your information

Tree admins can edit or delete people, photographs and documents at any time. To remove your account or a whole tree, or to ask what is held about you, email tejasweegaur111@gmail.com and it will be handled as promptly as possible.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
